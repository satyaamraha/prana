Prana

Privacy Policy

Last updated 21 November 2026 · Applies to the Prana website (prana.retrogradearc.com) and the Prana Android app.

In short: Prana works without an account and keeps your data on your device. If you choose to create an account, your practice data is stored in our database so it can sync between your devices. We don’t show ads, we don’t use analytics or tracking, and we never sell or share your data.

Data stored on your device

Your practice history, personal bests, programs, habit counters (including slips, triggers, notes, urges and check-ins), settings and any meditations you download for offline listening are stored in your browser’s storage on your device. You can export or erase it any time in Settings.

Optional account and sync

If you create an account we store:

DataWhy
Email address and a securely hashed passwordTo sign you in and send sign-in, confirmation and password-reset emails
A copy of your Prana data (practice log, personal bests, programs, habit counters, check-ins, settings)To sync your progress across your devices

This data is stored with Supabase, our database and authentication provider, and is protected so that only your signed-in account can read or change it. It is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and at rest.

Recorded meditations

Guided meditations in the “Recorded meditations” section are uploaded by Prana’s administrators and are public to all users. Regular users never upload audio. The microphone is only used inside the admin-only Meditation Studio, and only after you grant permission.

What we don’t do

  • No advertising, analytics, fingerprinting or third-party trackers.
  • No selling, renting or sharing of personal data.
  • No access to contacts, location, camera or files.

Health and habit information

Prana is a wellness tool, not a medical device. Habit counters and their notes are personal and sensitive; they stay on your device unless you sign in, in which case they sync privately to your account as described above. You can mark a counter as “discreet” to hide its name on the Today screen.

Deleting your account and data

Delete your account (from the app or this website):

  1. Open Prana and go to Settings → Account (or open prana.retrogradearc.com/#/account) and sign in.
  2. Tap Delete my account and type DELETE to confirm.

This permanently deletes your account, your email address and all synced data from our servers immediately. It cannot be undone. Data on your device is not touched; erase it with Settings → Erase all data.

Go to my account

If you can’t sign in, contact us using the developer email on Prana’s Google Play listing and we’ll delete your account within 30 days.

Children

Prana is not directed at children under 13 and we don’t knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes we’ll update the date above. Significant changes will be noted in the app.

Contact
